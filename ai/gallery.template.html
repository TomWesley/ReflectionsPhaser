How it works

The score is survival time — how long the AI keeps all five lasers away from the center core, capped at five minutes. Every board is server-generated with a fixed set of mirrors; the AI may only move and rotate them.

Its method is deliberately simple: an evolutionary hill-climb. Starting from the dealt layout, it repeatedly nudges or rotates a mirror, re-runs the exact game physics, and keeps the change only if the board survives at least as long. Exploration shrinks over time so it settles into a strong defense. Interesting finding: a single fixed strategy doesn't generalize across boards — the reflections are too chaotic — so the intelligence lives in optimizing each board on its own.
