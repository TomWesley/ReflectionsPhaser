Strategy

Today's board is the Corridor configuration — long rectangles, parallelograms and trapezoids. 5 lasers enter the field (3 from the right and 2 from the bottom), and the puzzle rates 2.2/10 (gentle).

Use the long edges to build lanes. Channel each laser into a corridor that carries it past the core and out, angling the channel walls tangential to the center.

General rule: your score is survival time, so you don't need a permanent solution — you need the beams to take the longest possible detour before any of them reaches the center. Keep mirrors out of the red exclusion ring, and remember lasers never fire perfectly straight across the board, so there's always an angle to exploit.
